Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum order quantity?

No minimum for standard products. For custom die-cast medals, minimum 25 pieces. Bulk pricing starts from 10+ units.

How fast can you deliver?

Standard orders ship in 5–7 business days. Express 48-hour service available for an additional ₹799 flat fee.

Can I see a proof before production?

Yes, always. We email a digital proof within 4 hours of order confirmation. Production starts only after your explicit approval.

Do you ship pan-India?

Yes! We ship to all 28 states via insured courier. International shipping available to 45+ countries on request.

Our 100% Satisfaction Promise

If your award doesn't meet our quality standards in any way, we will reproduce it completely free of charge. No questions asked. 30-day guarantee on every order.
