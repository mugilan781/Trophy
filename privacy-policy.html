Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information — in plain language.

Last updated: January 2026

1 Introduction

T&A Engraving Shop ("we", "our", "us") crafts premium trophies, medals, plaques and custom-engraved awards. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you browse our website, request a quote, place an order or contact us — and how we use, store and protect it. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to serve you:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number and company or organisation name when you submit a form, subscribe to our newsletter or create an account.
  • Order details — delivery address, award specifications, engraving text, logos and reference images you share for personalisation.
  • Account data — login credentials and saved engraving designs if you register.
  • Technical data — theme (light/dark) and language-direction preferences stored locally in your browser, plus basic anonymous usage statistics.

3 How We Use Your Information

  • To process quotes, confirm orders and arrange production and delivery.
  • To prepare engraving proofs and communicate revisions with you.
  • To respond to enquiries, typically within 2 business hours.
  • To send order updates and, only with your consent, newsletter offers and new-arrival announcements.
  • To improve our products, website experience and customer service.

4 Sharing & Disclosure

We never sell your personal information. We share it only when necessary: with trusted delivery partners to ship your awards, with payment processors to complete transactions securely, or when required by applicable law. Every partner is expected to handle your data responsibly.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses browser local storage (not advertising trackers) to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) and text direction (LTR/RTL). These values never leave your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

6 Data Security & Retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration or loss. Order records are retained for accounting and warranty purposes; marketing data is kept only while your consent remains active. You may request correction or deletion of your personal data at any time using the contact details below.

7 Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the website after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
