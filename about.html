Our Journey

37 Years of Milestones

From a small workshop in Mumbai to India's most trusted award manufacturer.

1987

The Beginning

Rajesh Trophywala opens a small engraving workshop in Dharavi, Mumbai, with two hand tools and a dream to honour achievement.

1995

First Corporate Contract

Wins a landmark contract with Tata Group for 500 customized executive plaques, catapulting T&A into the corporate awards space.

2003

Laser Technology Adopted

First engraver in Western India to introduce state-of-the-art fiber laser engraving, enabling unprecedented detail and volume capacity.

2012

ISO Certification

Achieved ISO 9001:2012 certification and expanded to a 10,000 sq ft purpose-built manufacturing facility in Andheri, Mumbai.

2019

National Award Winners

Honoured to supply awards for 3 consecutive National Games events, reaching over 15,000 athletes across India.

2024

Digital Engraving Studio

Launched our revolutionary online Live Engraving Preview tool — the first in India — allowing clients to design and proof awards instantly online.
